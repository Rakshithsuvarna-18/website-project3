About DKGOC Rooted in Dakshina Kannada
For Its People,

DKGOC Farmer Producer Company Limited is a farmer-owned and farmer-led organization committed to empowering agricultural communities through sustainable practices, collective growth, and market-driven solutions. Established with the objective of strengthening the economic well-being of farmers, the company works as a unified platform to enhance productivity, profitability, and long-term resilience in the agricultural sector.

How to Get Started

Becoming a DKGOC member and opening your account is simple — just 4 easy steps.

1

Register as Member

Visit your nearest DKGOC branch or call us. Complete a simple KYC form with Aadhaar, PAN, and a passport photo.

2

Choose Your Product

Select the savings or loan product that fits your needs — Daily Deposit, RD, FD, MIS, or a loan type.

3

Make Your Deposit

Deposit your first amount in-branch or hand it to our doorstep agent. Receive your passbook on the spot.

4

Watch It Grow

Track your balance via passbook or our agent visits. Earn interest regularly and collect your returns at maturity.
